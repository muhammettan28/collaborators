# Mobile review — 2026-10-28

## How it was checked

- **Signed-in screens** in Chrome (signed in). Chrome would not make its window
  narrower than **500×806**, which still gets the phone layout, so these were
  checked at 500px.
- **Signed-out screens** (`/`, `/signin/`, `/workspaces/discover/`, `?q=a`) in
  the Claude desktop app's built-in browser, which can emulate exact sizes:
  **390×844** and **360×740**. It is not signed in.

Screens checked (signed in, 500px): dashboard `/`, Recent `/?target=all_docs`,
`/?target=notifications`, Search `/workspaces/discover/` and `?q=a`,
`/account/profile/`, a page opened from Recent, the left panel, the bell popup,
and the "New" menu. Popups, menus and the panel were closed afterwards; no
settings or data were changed. Opening the page from Recent updated its
"last visited" time.

## What the script found

- **Sideways scrolling:** none, on any screen or size.
- **Sticking out:** only false alarms: the visually hidden "Collaborators" label,
  the drag handles in the closed left panel, and dnd-kit's live region. In a
  page, the table is wider than the screen but scrolls inside its own frame.
- **Small tap targets:**
  - Footer legal links, 15px tall (signed-out pages). **Fixed.**
  - Hierarchy note's "Learn more", 17px tall. **Fixed.**
  - "Sidebar on the right" switch in the panel, 34×18.
  - "Mark all as read" (21px) and "See all" (17px), in the bell popup and on the
    dashboard.
  - Page header's Reading/Editing toggle, 34×26; "Add labels", 19px tall.
  - Drag handles, 22×26; header logo link, 29px tall.
  - Inline links inside sentences (footer credits, "help build it on GitHub",
    "licence MIT"). These are normally allowed to be small.
  - Inputs reported at 19px are the inner field of 56px inputs: false alarm.
- **Small text:** the "MY" avatar initials (10px), and the floating labels of
  the profile form fields (11px, from the UI library).

## Seen on screenshots

- Bell popup: long notifications were cut off mid-word, with no "…". **Fixed.**
- The hierarchy note at the bottom of the dashboard (asked for during the run):
  on phones narrower than 500px the levels now stack vertically. **Done.**
- The left panel's content stays in the page when the panel is closed. It is
  not `inert`, so keyboard and screen-reader users can still reach it. The
  script sees its links as 12px wide.
- Signed-out home: the logo and button fade in for about 3s, partly because
  `/` redirects to `/home/` on the client and the animation starts again.
- Search screen: "Labels you are involved with appear here." is indented
  further than the text above it.

## Needs a decision

1. **Small controls in shared components** ("Mark all as read", "See all",
   the Reading/Editing toggle, "Add labels", the panel switch): make them at
   least 32px tall on phones? This touches the UI library's small button and
   switch sizes, so it needs a design choice.
2. **Closed left panel:** make it `inert` (or hide it) when closed?
3. **Notifications in the bell popup:** they now end with "…". Should they wrap
   onto two lines instead, since they are sentences?
4. **Signed-out home fade-in:** shorten it, or skip it after the redirect?
5. **Deploy:** the three commits above are waiting for
   `deploy/production/bin/deploy.sh 49.13.9.3`.
